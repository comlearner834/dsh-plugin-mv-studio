# 回家的路

> This 3-shot demo is a visual study: four lyric lines cross its three cuts.
> A real lyric MV uses one shot per line.

## Logline

A tired traveller waits for the last bus home; the night is long, but dawn
arrives and the road keeps going.

## One world

A paper-cut street at night, warm windows against blue rain. The same window
frame opens and closes the film; the bus at dawn rhymes with the window's
moon.

## Motif

The moon: a paper moon in the window, then a lamp behind rain, then the sun
that replaces it at dawn.
